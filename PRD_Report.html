Problem & Vision02

Trip planning is fragmented

Travelers today stitch together a plan from up to eight disconnected sources — with no single source of truth, and no way to verify what's current.

The Scattered Toolkit

Google MapsYouTubeTikTok Facebook GroupsInstagramWhatsApp Hotel SitesWord of Mouth

None of these sources are built for trip planning — each solves one narrow slice, leaving the traveler to manually reconcile outdated, contradictory, and unverifiable information.

Ten Recurring Pain Points

  • No single source of truth
  • Hidden locations are hard to discover
  • Poor budget estimation before departure
  • No transparent local pricing
  • Outdated route & destination info
  • Unknown road conditions and crowd levels
  • Lack of trusted local insight
  • Slow, multi-app planning process
  • No personalized itinerary generation
Vision — Combine AI, community contribution, maps, budgeting, and verified travel intelligence into one seamless app: Pakistan's most trusted travel operating system.

Product Goals & Their Measurable KPI

Goal Measurable KPI
Reduce trip planning time Median time to a saved itinerary < 15 minutes
Improve budget accuracy Estimated vs. actual spend within ±15%
Increase destination discovery 3+ new destinations viewed / active user / month
Improve traveler confidence Post-trip satisfaction score ≥ 4.3 / 5
Build the largest verified knowledge base 10,000+ verified POIs, 50,000+ reports in 18 months

MVP — Launch Scope

Northern Pakistan first: Murree, Naran-Kaghan, Hunza, Skardu.

  • Auth & Profiles
  • Discovery & Smart Search
  • Interactive Maps (core layers)
  • AI Trip Planner & Itinerary
  • Budget Estimator
  • Saved Trips + Offline Access
  • Reviews & Community Reports
  • Business Listings (self-serve)
  • Admin Dashboard & Moderation

V2 → V3 — What Follows

  • V2: Nationwide coverage, Expense Tracker, Hidden Gems / Viewpoints, Gamification & Badges, Business Dashboard analytics
  • V3: Advanced AI recommendations, predictive budgeting, early marketplace, tourism-board analytics, premium tier

Explicitly Out of Scope

  • Flight booking
  • International destinations
  • Full OTA / end-to-end paid booking
  • Complete transactional marketplace
  • Fully autonomous AI travel assistant
Core Features — MVP05

The nine features that carry the MVP

Each is fully specified in the master PRD (objectives, requirements, acceptance criteria, metrics). Summarized here.

Must Have

User Authentication

OTP, email, and social login with guest browsing — under 60 seconds to sign up.

Must Have

Discovery & Smart Search

Filter by budget, distance, season, and interest — results in <500ms.

Must Have

Interactive Maps

Layered roads, fuel, network, weather & emergency data on one map.

Must Have

AI Trip Planner

Day-by-day itinerary generated from budget, dates, and group type in <8 sec.

Must Have

Budget Estimator

Category-wise cost estimate from community-sourced, verified pricing.

Should Have

Expense Tracker

Log spend during the trip and auto-split it fairly across the group.

Must Have

Saved Trips & Offline

Itineraries and maps stay usable with zero connectivity for 7+ days.

Must Have

Community Reviews & Reports

Real-time road, crowd, and safety reports — live within 60 seconds.

Must Have

Business Listings

Self-serve listing creation with a document-based verification workflow.

Why this set — Together these nine features solve the two highest-frequency pain points directly: no single source of truth and poor budget accuracy — while offline support ensures the product still works where Pakistan's mountain network coverage doesn't.

System Architecture — Five Layers

Layer Role
Mobile App Offline-first iOS/Android client
API Gateway Auth, rate limiting, routing
Backend Services User, Trip, Community, Business
Data Layer PostgreSQL + Elasticsearch

Non-Negotiable NFRs

  • 99.9% uptime on auth, search & maps
  • P95 API response < 500ms
  • TLS 1.2+ everywhere; AES-256 at rest
  • Full offline access to saved trips
  • Urdu + English throughout
Release Roadmap07

Five phases to national scale

A 16+ month path from a five-destination MVP to a nationwide platform with an active business marketplace.

Months 01–04
Phase 1 — MVP
Core discovery, maps, AI trip planner, budget estimator, and community reviews for Northern Pakistan's highest-traffic destinations.
Months 05–07
Phase 2 — Community Expansion
Nationwide destination coverage, gamification & badges, deeper crowd/road reporting at scale.
Months 08–11
Phase 3 — Business Marketplace
Business listings at scale, verification workflows, inquiry management, first monetization via promoted listings.
Months 12–15
Phase 4 — AI Expansion
Advanced recommendations, predictive budgeting, natural-language trip planning.
Month 16+
Phase 5 — National Scale
Full national coverage, analytics products for tourism boards, exploratory international expansion.
